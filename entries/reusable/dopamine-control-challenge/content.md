## Description

A smaller, 30-day challenge supporting my ReferralChallenge: protect my attention so I spend more time learning, building, and moving. The aim is to make room for at least five hours of work in progress each day. “控制多巴胺” is my shorthand for changing my media habits.

这是支持 ReferralChallenge 的一个 30 天小挑战：减少随手刷视频，把注意力留给学习、创作和运动，争取每天至少有五小时推进实际工作。“控制多巴胺”在这里指的是管理自己的媒体使用习惯。

## User Guide

Read the rules each morning. Use a seven-minute timer when selecting study videos, then close YouTube. In the evening, check whether I followed the rules and record what I actually worked on.

每天早上看一遍规则；挑选学习视频时设定七分钟计时器，到时关闭 YouTube。晚上回顾有没有遵守规则，以及当天实际推进了什么。

## Content

### Rules / 规则

1. **No shorts in my apartment.** 不在公寓里刷任何短视频。
2. **At most seven minutes a day to select helpful YouTube study videos.** 每天最多用七分钟挑选有助于学习的 YouTube 视频。
3. **Watch downloaded videos only; no online video watching.** 只看已经下载的视频，不在线观看视频。挑选学习材料不等于开始在线观看。
4. **No games.** 不玩游戏。
5. **For leisure: read, listen to podcasts, exercise more, and meditate more.** 娱乐以阅读、听播客为主，多运动、多静坐。

### Daily review / 每日回顾

- Did I avoid shorts at home, online video watching, and games? 有没有做到在家不刷短视频、不在线观看视频、不玩游戏？
- Did I keep video selection within seven minutes? 挑选视频有没有控制在七分钟内？
- What did I learn or build? 今天学习或推进了什么？
- Did I make time for reading, podcasts, exercise, or meditation? 有没有留时间阅读、听播客、运动或静坐？
