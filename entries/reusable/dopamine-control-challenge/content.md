## Content

{{< language en >}}
### Rules

1. **No shorts in my apartment.**
2. **At most seven minutes a day to select helpful YouTube study videos.**
3. **Watch downloaded videos only; no online video watching.** Selecting study materials is not permission to start streaming them.
4. **No games.**
5. **For leisure: read, listen to podcasts, exercise more, and meditate more.**

### Daily review

- Did I avoid shorts at home, online video watching, and games?
- Did I keep video selection within seven minutes?
- What did I learn or build?
- Did I make time for reading, podcasts, exercise, or meditation?
{{< /language >}}

{{< language zh >}}
### 规则

1. **不在公寓里刷任何短视频。**
2. **每天最多用七分钟挑选有助于学习的 YouTube 视频。**
3. **只看已经下载的视频，不在线观看视频。** 挑选学习材料不等于开始在线观看。
4. **不玩游戏。**
5. **娱乐以阅读、听播客为主，多运动、多静坐。**

### 每日回顾

- 有没有做到在家不刷短视频、不在线观看视频、不玩游戏？
- 挑选视频有没有控制在七分钟内？
- 今天学习或推进了什么？
- 有没有留时间阅读、听播客、运动或静坐？
{{< /language >}}
